# Community conduct

Wristbeam welcomes different backgrounds, abilities, devices, and experience
levels. English and Chinese contributions are both welcome.

- Be considerate and give specific, constructive feedback.
- Discuss the work without personal attacks, harassment, threats, or slurs.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's identifying information,
  private messages, account data, or credentials without consent.
- Avoid sexualized content, spam, and repeated disruptive promotion.
- Accept reasonable moderation and make room for new contributors.

These expectations apply to project issues, PRs, Discussions, and other spaces
the project maintains. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close
disruptive threads, or restrict participation, proportionate to the behavior.
Where practical, explain the rule and allow a misunderstanding to be corrected;
serious harm can require immediate action.

For moderation concerns, use contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/shenxingy). For private concerns
when no suitable contact option is available, use GitHub's **Report abuse**
action. Do not post private details in a public report. Vulnerabilities use
the separate route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
